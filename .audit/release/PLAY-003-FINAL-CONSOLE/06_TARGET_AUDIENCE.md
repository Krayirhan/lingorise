# Target Audience — Final Owner Decision

## Decision (final, not re-litigated)

Not targeted: 5 and under — permanently excluded, never to be re-added silently.

## Product positioning (authoritative, used verbatim as the basis for every derived declaration)

"LingoRise is a general-audience English practice application for school-age children, teenagers and adults. It focuses on vocabulary, identifying correct English words and word forms, exercises, repetition and gamified practice rather than functioning primarily as a traditional course-based English teaching application."

## Consequence of including 6–8 and 9–12

Because the target audience genuinely includes children under 13, LingoRise becomes subject to Google Play's **Families Policy** — this is not avoidable by wording the declaration differently, and this run does not attempt to. See `07_FAMILIES_READINESS.md` for the dedicated audit this triggers.
